About Me#

I'm John GABARY

Professional Identity

I am a certified actuary driven by a strong passion for predictive modeling, statistical analysis, and risk management. My academic background blends mathematical rigor, economic insight, and operational relevance.

I design data-driven solutions to support strategic and sustainable decisions, building models that are robust, explainable, and useful. My approach bridges scientific precision and business understanding.

Vision & Commitment

Knowledge only has value when shared. That’s why I actively develop and share educational resources — especially through my personal project Actu-Basics, combining actuarial modeling and digital innovation.

I create interactive tools and accessible content to demystify actuarial science and data analytics. Sharing knowledge is also a way to grow: I am committed to continuous learning, creativity, and maximizing the social impact of my work.
